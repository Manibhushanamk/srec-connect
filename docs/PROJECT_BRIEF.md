## 2. Core Mission
To build a production-minded, mobile-first academic community and department operations platform that replaces disconnected spreadsheets, WhatsApp broadcasts, and bureaucratic ERP forms with a unified modern digital hub. 

The interface combines:
- **Instagram's familiarity:** Feed cards, stories/highlights, responsive bottom navigation, carousels, likes, and comments.
- **LinkedIn's credibility:** Academic and professional portfolios, skill endorsements, verified certificates, faculty research papers, patents, and FDPs.
- **Department operations:** Pre-provisioned Google OAuth, student roll-number lifecycle tracking, multi-tier achievement verification, confidential student grievances, faculty incident reports, and event QR check-in.
