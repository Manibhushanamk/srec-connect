# SREC Connect v3.0 — Roadmap

## Phases
- **Phase 0:** Discovery, Project Control & Architecture Lock (Current)
- **Phase 1:** Foundation & Design System (App shell, navigation, theme provider)
- **Phase 2:** Database & Authentication Engine (Neon + Prisma + Google OAuth RBAC)
- **Phase 3:** User & Academic Administration (Bulk CSV import, roll numbers, sections)
- **Phase 4:** Profiles & Portfolios (Student and Faculty LinkedIn-style profiles)
- **Phase 5:** Social Community & Feed (Instagram-style feed, max 2 images, Sharp WebP, R2)
- **Phase 6:** Achievements & Verification (Multi-tier review queue, Verified badges)
- **Phase 7:** Events & QR Attendance (Registration, seat limits, QR event check-in)
- **Phase 8:** Complaints & Discipline (Confidential grievances, right to respond, actions)
- **Phase 9:** Google Drive, Notices & Notifications (Certificates, announcements, push)
- **Phase 10:** Analytics & Public Portal (Recharts, SEO, global search)
- **Phase 11:** Production Readiness & Hardening (Full unit/E2E test suite, security audit)
